## What are Eternal items used for?
Eternal gear has stats equal to or better than Netherite.
For example, the Eternal Sword deals 15 damage, and the Eternal Axe deals 16, 
though they have very low durability.
Why, then, are they so expensive?
Because once their durability is depleted, they "go out" (become extinguished).
For tools and weapons, once they go out, 
their durability becomes effectively infinite, but their stats drop to the level of stone tools.
For armor, once it goes out, it gains infinite durability, 
but its stats drop to the level of gold gear.
You can repair Eternal gear using Blaze Rods.

Eternal Pearls and Eternal Totems differ from the original Totems of Undying and Ender Pearls.
Using an Eternal Totem to prevent death consumes its durability, 
and using an Eternal Pearl for teleportation also consumes its durability.
Both items can also be extinguished.

## Dragon-Gold Equipment
They are all very strong.
Crafting a Dragon Gold Ingot requires a Dragon Egg, 
which means you can only choose one out of three exceptionally powerful pieces of equipment.
Dragon Slayer / Red Dragon Sword, Dragon-Wing Armor / Dragon-Scale Armor, Dragon-Gold Totem / Dragon-Gold Talisman
You can only have one

## Upgrade Dragon-Gold Equipment
The Red Dragon Sword can be used for mining, 
the Dragon Wing Armor allows you to fly just as if you were in Creative Mode, 
and the Dragon Gold Talisman grants your talismans additional "cheat-death" (extra-life) charges.

## talisman
Press Z to raise the Talisman and block damage.
Even if you forget to raise it, 
the Gold/Starry Sky/Dragon Gold Talisman will save you from death 1–10 times.
If your durability is too low, you won't be granted immunity to death.

## Redstone Boots
Boots designed for Redstone enthusiasts to move between machines; 
they provide a speed boost when walking on Redstone blocks.
